How to play

Select one of your pieces, then choose a glowing destination square. Legal moves are highlighted.

  • Orbit: drag anywhere on the board
  • Zoom: mouse wheel or trackpad
  • Move: click a piece, then a square
  • Undo: rewind the last turn
  • V: reset camera
  • Esc: close this guide

The Warden searches ahead and grows stronger with each difficulty setting.
