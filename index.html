How to play

Move: click a piece, then click a glowing square. When a piece takes another, the camera swoops in to watch the fight.

Camera: drag to turn, scroll or pinch to zoom, W A S D / arrow keys to roam, Space to recentre.

V top view   F turn the board   U undo   K kill-cam   T storm   M sound   N menu

Music: use Settings → Choose a music file, or put a file called music.mp3 next to index.html and it plays by itself.
